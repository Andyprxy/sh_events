YOUR INFORMATION & YOUR RIGHTS

Privacy and Policy

S&H Events & Projects Consulting (Pty) Ltd uses your contact details, booking information and any driver’s licence you provide to assess your rental request, verify the driver and arrange your booking.

Our data retention schedule

Unsuccessful or abandoned requests · 30–90 days
For requests that do not result in a lease, personal details and uploaded documents are scheduled for deletion within 30–90 days after the request is declined, cancelled or last active, unless a lawful reason requires longer retention.

Completed leases · generally 3–5 years
Necessary lease, driver-verification and transaction records are retained in a restricted archive for tax administration, traffic fines, insurance, claims and fraud prevention. The period depends on the record and the applicable legal requirement; it is not a blanket deadline for every document.

Tax records · at least 5 years where required
For filed returns, the five-year period generally runs from submission of the relevant tax return, rather than the vehicle’s return date. Other statutory retention periods may apply.

Deletion when retention ends
Records are scheduled for secure deletion or irreversible de-identification once the applicable period expires and no legal hold or other lawful retention requirement remains.

We are committed to handling your information confidentially, with appropriate safeguards against unauthorised access. Access is limited to authorised staff and service providers who need it to process your booking.

How your information is handled

Limited use. Your licence is used for driver verification and rental administration, not marketing. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention exceptions. Deletion is paused for records needed for outstanding traffic fines, insurance claims, disputes, audits, investigations, court orders or other legal obligations. Access remains restricted. Retention is reviewed and records are deleted when both the applicable period and the lawful reason for the hold have ended. Only information necessary for the relevant purpose is retained; licence copies are not kept indefinitely simply because a booking occurred.

Your choice. The licence upload is optional at this request stage; verification may be needed before the rental is confirmed. Please upload only the requested licence, with the driver’s permission if you are booking on their behalf. Without agreement to the processing described here, you cannot submit this online request.

Your rights. You may request access to, correction or deletion of your information, object to processing or withdraw consent, subject to lawful retention requirements. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully completed and may affect our ability to arrange the rental.

For privacy requests, contact S&H through our website. You can also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.
