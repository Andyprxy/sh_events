Your privacy matters

S&H Events & Projects Consulting (Pty) Ltd uses your contact details, booking information and any driver’s licence you provide to assess your rental request, verify the driver and arrange your booking.

30-day retention policy
Our policy is to delete your uploaded licence and personal booking information within 30 days after your rental ends, or within 30 days of cancellation or a declined request. S&H does not keep these details indefinitely.

We are committed to handling your information confidentially, with appropriate safeguards against unauthorised access. Access is limited to authorised staff and service providers who need it to process your booking.

How your information is handled

Limited use. Your licence is used for driver verification and rental administration, not marketing. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention exceptions. Only records required by law or reasonably needed for an unresolved claim or dispute may be retained for longer. These remain restricted and are deleted when that reason no longer applies.

Your choice. The licence upload is optional at this request stage; verification may be needed before the rental is confirmed. Please upload only the requested licence, with the driver’s permission if you are booking on their behalf. Without agreement to the processing described here, you cannot submit this online request.

Your rights. You may request access to, correction or deletion of your information, object to processing or withdraw consent, subject to lawful retention requirements. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully completed and may affect our ability to arrange the rental.

For privacy requests, contact S&H through our website. You can also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.
